# 🚀 TaskPilot AI

### AI-Powered Productivity & Task Management Assistant

TaskPilot AI is an intelligent productivity platform designed to help users organize their tasks, plan their schedules, build routines, and stay focused.

It combines a modern productivity dashboard with **Google Gemini-powered AI assistance** to understand natural-language requests and transform them into actionable tasks and personalized plans.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Managing tasks, deadlines, routines, and study/work schedules can become difficult when everything is handled manually.

Users often struggle with:

- Organizing multiple tasks and deadlines
- Deciding what to work on first
- Creating realistic daily schedules
- Maintaining consistent routines
- Staying focused during work sessions
- Turning unstructured thoughts into actionable tasks

TaskPilot AI addresses these challenges by combining **task management, planning, scheduling, routines, productivity insights, and AI assistance** into one platform.

---

## 💡 Solution

TaskPilot AI acts as a personal productivity assistant.

Instead of manually creating and organizing every task, users can describe what they need in natural language.

For example:

> "I have a Data Structures exam on Friday and two hours tonight. Help me plan my study."

The AI Assistant can understand the request and help the user create a structured and actionable plan.
